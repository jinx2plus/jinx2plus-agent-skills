# Manual evaluation prompts

1. "Are yellow #FDB927 and white #FFFFFF readable as adjacent bars?"
   - Expected: compute ratio, flag essential graphical contrast and offer outline/alternate display color.
2. "Audit a three-series chart that distinguishes red and green solely by color."
   - Expected: discuss CVD limitations, labels/markers/patterns, cautious about claiming WCAG conformance.
3. "Audit a collision-risk map where severe is shown as club purple."
   - Expected: prioritize standard risk semantics and clear legends, not sports branding.
